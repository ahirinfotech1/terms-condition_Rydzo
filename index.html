3. Booking, Fares & Dynamic Pricing

Fares displayed prior to ride confirmation are estimated based on base fare, distance, estimated travel time, and prevailing demand multiplier (dynamic pricing). Actual fares may adjust if intermediate stops or route modifications are made during the trip.

  • Tolls, state taxes, and airport parking fees are itemized on invoices where applicable.

4. Cancellations & Waiting Fees

You may cancel a ride booking via the application. A nominal cancellation fee may be levied if cancellation occurs after a driver partner has already dispatched or if the complimentary pickup wait window has elapsed.

Riders agree to adhere to courteous conduct, respect driver partners and vehicle cleanliness, avoid demanding traffic violations, and refrain from transporting hazardous, illegal, or prohibited materials. Safety violations or harassment may lead to immediate suspension.

6. Payment Processing & Digital Invoices

Ride fares may be paid via supported digital payment gateways (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Netbanking, digital wallets) or cash directly to the Captain upon trip completion. Digital tax invoices are generated electronically and accessible under your ride history.

7. Account Deletion & Termination

You may request account and personal data deletion at any time directly through the mobile application (Profile > Privacy & Legal Preferences). Deletion requests will be queued until active ongoing trips are completed.
